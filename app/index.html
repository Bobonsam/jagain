🔗 How Sentinel works

Two contracts, two chains, zero servers.

SentinelCallback (destination chain) holds your orders and performs the swap. SentinelReactive on the Reactive Network subscribes to the pair's Sync events, recomputes the price on every swap, ratchets trailing-stop peaks, and calls back through the Reactive callback proxy when a trigger is crossed. The callback re-verifies the price from live reserves before swapping, so a stale or forged trigger can never force a bad trade.
